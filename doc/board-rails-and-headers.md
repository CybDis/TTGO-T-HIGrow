# Board supply rails and header pinout (LilyGO T-HiGrow)

Reference notes derived from `images/schematic.pdf`, so this does not have to be
re-read from the PDF each time. Everything below is what the schematic shows;
where it disagrees with LilyGO's pinmap graphic (`images/T-Higrow.jpg`), the
disagreement is called out explicitly.

## Supply rails

The board has three supply nets that are easy to confuse. Two of them are
nominally 3.3 V but are generated by different regulators from different
sources.

| Net | Source | Supplies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `+5V` | Not a regulated 5 V rail. It is the system input rail: VBUS through `D7` (1N5819) when USB is connected, otherwise the cell through `Q5` (SI2307) / `Q3` (SI2301). On battery it sits at cell voltage, not 5 V. | Input of `U8` |
| `VDD3V3` | `U8` (AP2112K-3.3V, RT9013 as alternate), fed from `+5V` | ESP32, flash, CP2104 |
| `V3V` | `U4` (JC5333), 3.3 V linear regulator fed **directly from the cell node `BAT`** | TLC555 soil oscillator (`U11`), BH1750 (`U0`), BME280 (`U15`), the `P6`/`P8` sensor connectors, and the `VREF` / `-VREF` generation |

Two further rails are derived from `V3V` and feed the salt/EC front-end only:

- `VREF`, about +5 V: `U12` (HT7750S) boosts `V3V` through `L3` 47uH with `D8`
  (1N5817).
- `-VREF`, about -5 V: `U10` (LM2660) charge-pump inverter, `V+` on `VREF`.

`U5` (CD4060) runs between `VREF` and `-VREF` (its GND pin 8 and RST pin 12 are
tied to `-VREF`), as does `U6` (TL034), with `V+` on pin 4 and `V-` on pin 11.

### Consequence for the analog readings

`V3V` is regulated only while the cell stays above `3.3 V` plus `U4`'s dropout.
Below that, `V3V` follows the cell and both analog channels drift with it. This
is the mechanism analysed in `doc/2026-07-11-soil-battery-sag-analysis.md`.

`U4`'s input is on `BAT` with no switch in series, so `V3V` appears to be
powered continuously, including during deep sleep. `PWR_EN` (GPIO4) does not
gate the sensor rail: it drives `Q4` (PMBT3904), which pulls the gate of `Q3`
(SI2301) low. `Q3` sits between `BAT` and the system rail and bypasses its own
body diode when enabled, which is why readings are wrong unless GPIO4 is held
high.

## Analog channel to GPIO mapping

| Firmware | GPIO | ESP32 pin | Net | Front-end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `SOIL_PIN` | 32 | 12 | `Humi` | `U11` TLC555 astable on `V3V`, peak-detected by `D5` |
| `SALT_PIN` | 34 | 10 | `AD` | `U5` CD4060 plus `U6` TL034 absolute-value chain on `VREF` / `-VREF` |
| `BAT_ADC` | 33 | 13 | `BAT_ADC` | `R10` / `R78` divider, both 100K 1%, with `C26` 10nF |
| `USER_BUTTON` | 35 | 11 | `KEY1` | - |

Matches `src/main.cpp` and LilyGO's pinmap ("Fertility: IO34, Watering: IO32").

## Side headers

### `P1` (13P) - the header carrying GPIO36/37/38/39

| Pin | Net | Pin | Net |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `VDD3V3` | 7 | `IO14` |
| 2 | `GND` | 8 | `IO27` |
| 3 | `IO2` | 9 | `IO39` |
| 4 | `IO15` | 10 | `IO38` |
| 5 | `IO13` | 11 | `IO37` |
| 6 | `IO12` | 12 | `IO36` |
|  |  | 13, 14 | not connected |

### `P2` (12P) - the header carrying the `5V` pin

| Pin | Net | Pin | Net |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `+5V` | 7 | `IO5` |
| 2 | `GND` | 8 | `IO17` |
| 3 | `VDD3V3` | 9 | `IO23` |
| 4 | `GND` | 10 | `GND` |
| 5 | `IO19` | 11 | `GND` |
| 6 | `IO18` | 12 | `GND` |

**Both side-header 3.3 V pins are the same net.** `P1` pin 1 and `P2` pin 3 are
both `VDD3V3`, which is why the silkscreen reads `M3V` on both. LilyGO's pinmap
graphic labels one of them "3V3" and the other "3V"; that is an inconsistency in
the graphic, not two different voltages. Neither pin carries `V3V`.

## Sensor connectors

### `P6` (4P) - external sensor connector on `IO21` / `IO22`

| Pin | Net |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | `V3V` |
| 2 | `IO21` |
| 3 | `IO22` |
| 4 | `GND` |

LilyGO labels this connector `3V3 / 21 / 22 / GND` and marks it as UART/I2C, but
the on-board BH1750 and BME280 use `IO25` / `IO26`, not `IO21` / `IO22`. In this
firmware `IO21` is `DS18B20_PIN`, so the external DS18B20 goes here.

### `P8` (4P) - `DATA` connector

| Pin | Net |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | `V3V` |
| 2 | `DATA`, pulled up to `V3V` by `R00` 4.7K |
| 3 | not connected |
| 4 | `GND` |

On the board this is the pad group silkscreened `3V3 / DA / NC / G`. The 4.7K
pull-up to `V3V` fits a 1-Wire or single-wire sensor line. Which GPIO the `DATA`
net terminates on could not be resolved reliably from the PDF geometry, so
confirm it by measurement before relying on it.

`P6` pin 1 and `P8` pin 1 are the only externally accessible points on the
`V3V` rail.

## Battery connector

`P5` (the LiPo connector) pin 1 goes through `R43` to the `BAT` net, which also
carries the `TP4054` charger output, the `R10`/`R78` divider, `Q3`'s drain and
`U4`'s input. `(3.7V~~4.2V)` is annotated on the schematic at the `VBAT` net.

## Known errors in `images/T-Higrow.jpg`

- The two `M3V` pins are annotated "3V3" and "3V" although they are the same
  `VDD3V3` net.
- `P2` pin 6 is `IO18` in the schematic; the graphic labels that position
  "13" / GPIO13. GPIO13 is on `P1` pin 5.

When it matters, trust the schematic and a continuity measurement rather than
the graphic.
